Movie recommendations you can check

Tell us a few films you love.
We’ll show you what to watch next, and why.

Cinegraph connects films through 100,000 real ratings. When people who loved your favourites also loved another film, that link becomes a recommendation, and you can see the exact connection behind it.

No account. Takes about two minutes. Your picks stay in this tab.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick favourites

    You see five well-known films at a time. Choose the one you like most, or skip a round.

  2. 2

    We follow the graph

    We look for films that the same MovieLens raters also scored 4★ or higher.

  3. 3

    See the reason

    Every suggestion has a “Why?” button showing which of your picks it is connected to, and through how many people.
